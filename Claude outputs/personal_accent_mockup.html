Today's purple #667eea 3.66—— —— fails its own text
The violet is not a new colour. The Personal side already carries 67 purple literals across 11 templates — #667eea at hue 271 and #764ba2 at hue 305. The proposed accent sits at hue 295, between them, at the teal's own lightness and chroma. It makes the identity those pages already have into a token — and fixes it: #667eea carries white text at 3.66 and fails, which is what D9 found on all five avatars. The token passes at 5.27.
Contrast is WCAG against white text; the title in each pop-up header is measured against the lightest stop of its gradient. Distances are OKLab ΔE × 100, the same metric the series scale is validated with. Every component above is rebuilt from base.html's own rules — only the four accent tokens change between the two panels.
