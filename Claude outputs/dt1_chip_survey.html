The Status Chips on Home and Notifications

Before I convert those 13 tables, one thing needs your eye. Two of the three status colours are solid fills today. The house pill is a soft tint with a matching outline, so converting does not just round the corners - it turns the volume down. Here are the seven chips those pages draw, in all three treatments.

The case against Option 2, so you have both sides: a solid red block is louder than a soft pill, and Vacant on a dashboard is a thing you want to notice. If you would rather those two keep shouting, say so and I will leave the red and teal as fills and convert only the shape - but then those two pages keep a status vocabulary of their own, which is the thing this round is meant to end.

What else the survey found

  
The six panels exist twiceHome and Notifications build the same six tables - Vacant, Expiring, Declined, Overdue, Approvals, Payments - from two separate copies of the code. One uses string joining, the other template literals. Home has a seventh, inside the income pop-up.
So does the stylesheetBoth pages declare the same four .status-badge rules, word for word. The duplication is the round.
Seven first cells have no labelEvery other cell carries a data-label so the phone card can print its heading. The first one does not, on all seven home tables.
One tone is decided in JavaScriptThe income pop-up picks between amber and teal with a ternary on b.tag === 'at_risk'. That stays - it is data, not drift - but it means the round cannot be a blind find-and-replace.
