AThe two pages that carry a second table standard

Home and Notifications build their tables in JavaScript, as strings, against a page-local .data-table stylesheet - a hand-rolled copy of the house table standard, living beside it. No table suite in this project can see any of it. Banked as DT-1 when NV-1b converted the title deeds table.

BDoes the title deeds Status column earn its place?

TD-2 flagged this and did not decide it. The column says only Available or Not Uploaded - and the action strip beside it already says the same thing, because View is drawn as a greyed twin when there is nothing attached.

This one is your taste, not a standard. For removing it: no row can disagree with its own action strip, so the column cannot tell you anything the icons have not. For keeping it: a word is faster to scan down a long list than the absence of colour in an icon, and on a phone the pill reads at a glance where a greyed icon does not. Small round either way - one page, one patcher, one suite.
