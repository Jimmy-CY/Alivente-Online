What is this page?

Personal Notification Settings is the central place where you decide who in your family should receive personal email notifications from the system.

There are two types of personal notifications you can configure here:

  • Celebration Reminders — Daily emails that remind you about upcoming birthdays, namedays and anniversaries.
  • Document Expiry Alerts — Twice-monthly emails that warn you about passports and IDs that are about to expire.
Why is this important?
Without email addresses configured here, no one will receive these notifications — even if a celebration or document expiry date matches today.
Looking for business / property notifications? Things like Daily Property Reports, Lease Reminders, Invoice and Expense alerts, and the Friday Status Report are configured separately under Administration → Notification Settings.
Understanding TO and CC

Each notification type has two recipient fields, just like a normal email:

TO: Primary Recipients

The main people who should receive — and act on — the notification.

Example: demetri@gmail.com, angy@gmail.com, erene@gmail.com

CC: Carbon Copy Recipients (optional)

Additional people who should be kept informed but don't necessarily need to take action. Leave blank if not needed.

Example: angmaniasbakery@gmail.com

Email Format
  • Enter one or many email addresses per field.
  • Separate multiple addresses with a comma — for example email1@example.com, email2@example.com.
  • Spaces after commas are fine and will be ignored.
Saving Changes

Each section (Celebration Reminders, Document Expiry Alerts) has its own Save button. After editing a section you must click Save in that section — changes in one section are independent of the other and are not saved together.

Celebration Reminders

What it does: Sends email reminders for birthdays, namedays and anniversaries.

When it sends: Every morning at 9:00 AM, but only on days where there is a matching event (1 week before, 1 day before, or same-day — based on each event's settings in the Celebrations module).

Who is reminded: The recipients you list here, but only if the family member is also ticked on that specific event in the Celebrations module.

TO field hint: Family members who should be reminded

Document Expiry Alerts

What it does: Warns you about passports and IDs that are about to expire (or have already expired).

When it sends: Twice a month — on the 1st and 15th.

What's included: Any passport or ID expiring within the next 6 months, plus any that have already expired.

TO field hint: Family members who manage documents

Related modules: Celebrations, Passports / Documents.
Best Practices
  • Add multiple recipients — if one person is on holiday, others will still see the notification.
  • Use CC for awareness — keep someone in the loop without making them responsible for action.
  • Save each section separately — don't forget to click the Save button in the section you've edited.
  • Test with your own email first — before adding others, save with just your address to confirm you receive the next scheduled notification.
  • Keep it tidy — remove old or obsolete email addresses to avoid noise.
  • Pair with the Celebrations module — adding an email here doesn't automatically tick that person on every event. You must also tick "Notify These People" on each celebration for them to be included.
Not receiving a celebration notification? Check three things:
  1. Is the email address registered here (in Notification Settings)?
  2. Is the person ticked on the specific event in Celebrations?
  3. Is the timing (1 week / 1 day / same day) enabled on that event?
All three conditions must be true for the email to be sent.
