Administration Notification Settings — User Guide
Manage email recipients for property management and operational notifications

What is this page?

Administration Notification Settings is the central place where you decide who in the organisation receives operational email notifications — covering property management, leases, invoices, expenses and the weekly status report.

There are eight notification types you can configure on this page:

  1. Daily Property Management Report
  2. New Lease Upload Reminders
  3. Invoice Marked as Paid
  4. Expense Needs Approval
  5. Expense Approved
  6. Expense Paid
  7. Friday Status Report (Submitted by Supervisor)
  8. Friday Status Report (Submitted by Staff)
Why is this important?
Each notification represents a real business event — an invoice paid, an expense awaiting approval, a lease about to be uploaded. Configuring the right recipients for each one ensures the right person sees the right alert at the right time.
Looking for personal notifications? Birthday and anniversary reminders, plus passport / ID expiry alerts, are configured separately under Personal → Notification Settings.
Understanding TO and CC

Each of the eight notification types has the same two recipient fields, just like a normal email:

TO: Primary Recipients

The main person (or people) responsible for acting on the notification.

Example: stella.simitopoulos@alivente.com

CC: Carbon Copy Recipients (optional)

Additional people who should be kept informed for visibility but don't need to take action. Leave blank if not needed.

Example: angmaniasbakery@gmail.com

Email Format
  • Enter one or many email addresses per field.
  • Separate multiple addresses with a comma — for example email1@example.com, email2@example.com.
  • Spaces after commas are fine and will be ignored.
Saving Changes

Each notification section has its own Save button. After editing a section you must click Save in that section — the eight sections are saved independently of each other.

Tip: If you edit several sections, click Save in each one before navigating away. Unsaved changes will be lost.
1. Daily Property Management Report

What it does: Automated daily report covering vacant properties, expiring leases and overdue invoices.

When it sends: Once a day, automatically.

Typical recipient: Property managers and supervisors who need to take daily operational action.

2. New Lease Upload Reminders

What it does: Urgent notifications when a new lease agreement needs to be uploaded into the system.

Typical recipient: The person responsible for uploading and managing lease documents.

3. Invoice Marked as Paid

What it does: Triggered when an invoice is marked as paid in the system.

Typical recipient: The person who tracks invoice payments and reconciles accounts.

4. Expense Needs Approval

What it does: Triggered when a non-supervisor user creates an expense that requires approval.

Typical recipient: The person who approves expenses (usually a supervisor).

5. Expense Approved

What it does: Triggered when an expense has been approved and is now pending payment.

Typical recipient: The person who processes payments — this is their cue to pay.

6. Expense Paid

What it does: Triggered when an expense has been marked as paid in the system.

Typical recipient: The person who tracks completed expenses for record-keeping.

7. Friday Status Report (Submitted by Supervisor)

What it does: Triggered when a supervisor submits the Friday Status Report.

Typical recipient: The main recipient of the supervisor's weekly report (usually senior management or ownership).

8. Friday Status Report (Submitted by Staff)

What it does: Triggered when a non-supervisor staff member submits the Friday Status Report.

Typical recipient: The supervisor (so they can review the staff report before passing it up the chain).

How notifications support business processes

Several notifications work together as a chain — configuring them correctly turns the system into an end-to-end alerting pipeline.

Expense Approval Chain
  1. Staff member creates an expense → Expense Needs Approval notifies the approver.
  2. Approver approves it → Expense Approved notifies the payer.
  3. Payer pays it → Expense Paid notifies the tracker for record-keeping.
Friday Status Report Chain
  1. Staff submit their reports → Friday Status Report (Staff) notifies the supervisor for review.
  2. Supervisor submits the consolidated report → Friday Status Report (Supervisor) notifies senior management.
Daily Operational Awareness

The Daily Property Management Report and New Lease Upload Reminders work as proactive nudges — the system tells managers what needs doing rather than waiting for them to log in and check.

Design tip: Set different people in TO at each step of a chain. If the same person appears in TO at every step they'll get email fatigue and start ignoring alerts. Use CC for visibility, TO for action.
Best Practices
  • Match the recipient to the role — the person in TO should be the one who actually does something with the notification, not a generic inbox.
  • Always have a backup — add a second TO recipient (or CC) so cover is in place when the primary person is out.
  • Use CC for visibility — senior managers often want to know what's happening without being on the hook to act.
  • Save each section separately — each of the eight notifications has its own Save button.
  • Avoid email fatigue — don't put the same person in TO across all eight notifications, or they'll start ignoring everything.
  • Test changes — after editing a section, trigger the relevant action (e.g. mark a test invoice as paid) to confirm the notification reaches the right inbox.
  • Review periodically — staff change roles, leave, or take on new responsibilities. Review these settings every few months.
Not receiving an expected notification? Check three things:
  1. Is the email address registered here in the relevant section?
  2. Did you click Save on that specific section after editing?
  3. Is the email being filtered into spam? Whitelist the sending address.
