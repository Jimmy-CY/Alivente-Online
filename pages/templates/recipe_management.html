What is Recipe Management?

Recipe Management is your complete digital cookbook. Store, browse, and cook from your entire recipe collection. Plan your weekly meals, generate shopping lists, and use smart tools like the ingredient matcher and cooking calculator.

Key Features
3 View Modes
Browse in Grid, List, or Book (ring-binder) view.
Smart Filters
Filter by course, category, protein, author, or search by name or ingredient.
Shopping Lists
Per-recipe or whole meal-plan shopping lists with pantry deduction.
Cooking Calculator
Enter serving time and meat weight — get a full cooking schedule.
What Can I Make?
Select ingredients you have and find recipes with % match scores.
Meal Plans
Plan up to 7 days of meals and generate a weekly shopping list.
AI Import
Paste any recipe and let AI parse it into a structured format automatically.
Favourites
Mark recipes as favourites and filter to view only your favourite collection.
Browsing Your Recipe Collection

List
Compact rows with thumbnail, name, tags, time and difficulty.
Book
Ring-binder style. 3, 6 or 9 per page. Click a recipe for full detail.

Viewing a Recipe

The recipe header shows prep time, cook time, total time, servings, difficulty, course/category/protein tags and the author. Ingredients are displayed in two columns with weight equivalents shown in grey. Instructions are numbered and grouped by stage.

Shopping List — 3 Steps
Step 1 — Servings: Set how many servings you need. All ingredient quantities scale automatically.
Step 2 — Pantry review: Tick any ingredients you already have at home — they are excluded from the list.
Step 3 — Your list: Final shopping list grouped by category. Share via Print, Copy, Email, or WhatsApp.
Calculate Cooking Times
For braai/BBQ and oven recipes. Enter your desired serving time and meat weight (grams). The calculator works backwards to produce a full schedule:
Light Fire — when to start the fire
Start Cooking — when to put the meat on
Finish Cooking / Start Resting
Start Cutting & Making Sauce
Serving Time — your target time
The schedule can also be accessed directly from the Recipe View page as a pop-up calculator using the serving time and meat weight you enter. Click Print Schedule to keep a copy.
View Document
Opens the attached PDF document (if one has been uploaded for this recipe) in the browser.
Print Recipe
Sends the recipe to your printer in a clean, formatted layout.
Adding and Editing Recipes

Click Add Recipe → Manual Entry to create a recipe from scratch, or Add Recipe → AI Import to paste any recipe text and let AI structure it for you. Click the yellow Edit button on any recipe to update it.

1 Basic Information
Recipe name, description, author, and photo (JPG/PNG/WebP, max 5MB, recommended 800×600px).
2 Time & Servings
Prep time, cook time (total is calculated automatically), and number of servings.
3 Classification
Assign one or more Courses and Categories (multi-select with chips). Set difficulty level. You can add new courses or categories on the fly.
4 Dietary Information
Tick "Vegetarian" or select one or more proteins. You can add new proteins on the fly.
5 Ingredients
Enter quantity, measurement unit, ingredient name, preparation note, and group (e.g. "Herb Mix"). Ingredient fields are colour-coded: green = valid existing ingredient, red = new item that will be added. Rows can be reordered by dragging.
6 Instructions
Numbered steps with optional group headings (e.g. "Ordering", "Pre-Preparation", "Cooking"). Steps can be reordered by dragging. Use Check Spelling to run a spell-check on all instruction text.
7 Document Attachment
Upload a PDF, Word, Excel, JPG or PNG (max 5MB). If a document already exists, choose to Replace it or Add to existing (merges PDFs). You can also delete the current document.
8 Cooking Calculator (optional)
For braai/BBQ or oven recipes. Select cooking method and enter: Serving Time, Fire Lighting Time, Meat Weight, Resting Time, Cutting & Sauce time, Rate 1 and Rate 2 (mins per 500g). The live cooking schedule updates as you type and is saved with the recipe.
What Can I Make?

Click What Can I Make? from the main toolbar. Select the ingredients you currently have at home and the system finds all recipes you can make — ranked by how well your ingredients match each recipe.

Selecting ingredients: Ingredients are grouped by category (Baking, Dairy, Vegetables, Meat, etc.). Click a category header to expand it. Tick individual ingredients or use Select All to tick the whole category. Use the search box to find a specific ingredient quickly.
Automatic inclusions: Herbs, spices, oil, and water are automatically included in every search — you don't need to tick them.
Results: Each matching recipe shows a % match score, how many ingredients you have vs the total, and a list of missing ingredients. Missing proteins are highlighted in red. Click any result to open the full recipe.
Modifying selections: After results are shown, click "Click to modify selections" at the top to go back and adjust your ingredient choices without starting over.

Meal Plans

Click Meal Plans from the toolbar to manage your weekly meal schedules. View as a list or a calendar. Each plan has a name, a date range of 1–7 days, and recipes assigned to each day.

Creating a Meal Plan: Give it a name and set a start and end date (1–7 days). Then assign recipes to each day and set the number of servings per recipe.
Calendar View: A mini calendar shows dots on days that have meals planned. Click a day to view and manage that day's recipes. Add or remove recipes directly from the calendar view.
Meal Plan Shopping List: Click the green List button on any meal plan. The system consolidates all ingredients from every recipe across all days into one master shopping list — quantities are totalled across recipes. Tick off what you already have in your pantry and only the remaining items appear on the final list. Share via Print, Copy, Email or WhatsApp.
Duplicate a Meal Plan: Creates a copy with a new name and new start date. The plan keeps the same duration and all the same recipes. Optionally tick Delete original after duplicating to effectively shift a plan to new dates.
Tip: Use the Meal Plan Shopping List before your weekly grocery shop — it automatically combines all ingredient quantities across all recipes for the week into one consolidated list.
Settings

The Settings dropdown contains four management pages that control how the system handles ingredients, units and conversions — all of which affect shopping list aggregation.

Unit Conversions
Defines how units convert to each other for shopping list consolidation. For example, 1 bag → 4 kg or 1 cup → 240 ml.

Generic conversions apply to all ingredients (e.g. 1 cup = 240ml for any liquid).
Specific conversions apply to one ingredient only (e.g. 1 cup Flour = 125g).

The Missing Conversions button highlights combinations that exist in your recipes but have no conversion defined yet.
Categories
Manages ingredient categories (Baking, Dairy, Vegetables, Meat, etc.). Categories are used to group ingredients in the What Can I Make? selector and on shopping lists. Each category shows how many ingredients use it. You can add, rename, or delete categories.
Units (Measurement Units)
Manages all measurement units available in the recipe editor (cup, tablespoon, gram, kilogram, etc.). Each unit has a singular and plural form, an optional abbreviation, and a type: Weight, Volume, Count, or Other. The type determines rounding behaviour in shopping lists.
Ingredients (Shopping Units)
Sets the preferred shopping unit for specific ingredients. When a shopping list is generated, ingredients with a defined shopping unit are displayed in that unit (e.g. Butter always shown in grams, Garlic always in cloves). This ensures shopping lists are presented in the most practical unit for each ingredient.
Note: These settings affect how shopping lists are calculated and how units are aggregated. If your shopping list shows unexpected quantities, check Unit Conversions for missing or incorrect conversion rules.
Tips and Best Practices
Use AI Import to add recipes fast
Find a recipe online, copy the text, and paste it into AI Import. The system structures it automatically — just review and save. Much faster than manual entry.
Use ingredient and instruction groups
Group related ingredients (e.g. "Herb Mix", "For the Sauce") and group instruction steps (e.g. "Pre-Preparation", "Cooking") to make complex recipes easy to follow.
Set up the Cooking Calculator for braai recipes
For any braai, BBQ or oven roast recipe, fill in the cooking calculator fields when editing. Then on the day, just enter the meat weight and target serving time to get the full schedule.
Use Meal Plans for your weekly shop
Build a 7-day meal plan, then generate the shopping list. Tick off what's in your pantry and the list automatically contains only what you need to buy — nothing more.
Keep Unit Conversions up to date
Check Settings → Unit Conversions → Missing Conversions regularly. Any undefined conversions prevent shopping list quantities from being properly aggregated across recipes.
Duplicate recipes to create variations
Use the Duplicate button to copy a recipe, then tweak the copy. Great for creating vegetarian versions, different portion sizes, or regional variations without losing the original.
Use Book View in the kitchen
Switch to Book View for a clean, print-style reading experience. Navigate between recipes with the arrows and read ingredients and instructions side by side — ideal when cooking.
