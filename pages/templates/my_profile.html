What is the My Profile page?

My Profile is where you manage your personal account settings within Alivente Online. The page is split into three sections:

  • Profile Photo — the picture (or initials) that represent you in the system
  • Personal Information — your name, email address and username
  • Menu Preference — how you want the navigation to appear (top bar or left sidebar)
The User Meta box

Just below the page header you'll see a small grey box showing three pieces of read-only info:

  • Username — your login name
  • Role — either Superuser (administrator) or User
  • Joined — the date your account was created

These values cannot be changed from this page.

One Save button covers everything: You can edit any combination of the three sections at once. A single click on Save Profile at the bottom commits all your changes together.
What appears in your profile circle?

The system follows a simple three-step fallback when deciding what to show in the round profile area:

1. Uploaded photo (takes priority)

If you have uploaded a photo, it is shown. The photo overrides everything else.

2. Initials from your name

If you have no photo but you have filled in both First Name and Last Name in Personal Information, the system shows your initials in the coloured circle — for example "Demetri Manias" becomes DM.

3. First letter of your username (fallback)

If you have no photo and your name fields are incomplete (one or both blank), the system falls back to the first letter of your username — for example user admin shows as A.

Want initials instead of a photo? Don't upload anything — just fill in both First Name and Last Name in Personal Information and click Save. Your initials will appear automatically.
Uploading a photo

Click the Upload Photo button to choose an image from your device. A preview appears immediately, but the photo is only saved once you click Save Profile.

Requirements:

  • Format: JPG, PNG or GIF
  • Maximum size: 2 MB
  • Square images work best (the photo is shown as a circle)
Remove current photo

Once you have a photo uploaded, a Remove current photo checkbox appears beneath the upload button. Tick it and click Save Profile to revert back to the initials (or username letter) placeholder.

Personal Information fields
First Name & Last Name

Your full display name. These appear wherever your activity is shown in the system — for example next to expenses you've created or reports you've submitted.

Bonus: filling in both fields also generates your profile initials (e.g. DM for Demetri Manias) when you don't have a photo uploaded.

Email Address

The email address linked to your account. This is important — it's the address that system notifications are sent to (when configured to do so in Notification Settings).

Keep this up to date. If your work email changes, update it here so you don't miss anything.

Username (read-only)

Your login name, shown in grey because it cannot be changed from this page. Usernames are set once at account creation and need an administrator to change.

Need to change your username? Contact your system administrator. Username changes affect login credentials and aren't done lightly.
Choosing your navigation style

Alivente Online supports two navigation styles. Pick whichever suits your screen size and workflow — you can switch any time.

Top Menu (default)

A horizontal navigation bar runs across the top of every page. Best for wider screens (laptops, desktops, large monitors) where you have plenty of horizontal space.

Sidebar

A collapsible menu sits on the left-hand side of the screen. Better for narrower screens, multi-window setups, or anyone who prefers a vertical menu they can hide when not needed.

Your choice persists: Once you save, the menu style stays the same on every login from any device until you change it again.
Best Practices
  • Fill in both First and Last Name — even if you don't want to upload a photo, completing both name fields gives you a tidy two-letter initial (e.g. DM) instead of a single username letter.
  • Keep your email up to date — it's where notifications and system alerts are sent. An out-of-date email means you'll miss important updates.
  • Upload a recognisable photo — especially useful for shared / team accounts so colleagues can quickly identify who's who.
  • Use a square photo — the photo is displayed inside a circular frame, so square images crop best. Portrait or landscape photos may have parts cut off.
  • Pick the menu that fits your screen — Top Menu works well on a 27-inch desktop monitor, while Sidebar is more comfortable on a narrow laptop or when you have multiple windows side-by-side.
  • Always click Save Profile — changes aren't applied until you save. Clicking Cancel or Back discards anything you've edited but not saved.
Photo not uploading? Check that the file is under 2 MB and in JPG, PNG or GIF format. Other formats (HEIC, BMP, TIFF, etc.) won't be accepted.
