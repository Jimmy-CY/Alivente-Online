What is Passport / ID Management?

The Passport / ID Management module is your centralised store for all identity and travel documents belonging to family members or associates. You can upload, view, edit, and delete documents, and the system automatically monitors expiry dates and sends you timely notifications.

Key Features
Multiple Document Types
Store Passports, ID Cards, Driver's Licences, ARC cards, and more — all in one place.
View Documents
View uploaded document scans or PDFs directly in the browser without downloading.
Expiry Alerts
Expiry dates shown in red when a document is expiring within 6 months or has already expired.
Email Notifications
Automatic email alerts on the 1st and 15th of every month for documents expiring soon or already expired.
Filtering
Filter by holder name, document type, country of issue, and status to quickly find any document.
Multiple Holders
Manage documents for multiple family members or associates — all in a single, searchable list.
Important: Expiry dates shown in red indicate the document has already expired or will expire within 6 months. These are included in the automated email sent on the 1st and 15th of every month.
Understanding the Document List

Each row represents one document. All holders and their documents are shown together in a single list.

ColumnDescription
Holder NameThe person this document belongs to.
Document TypeColour-coded badge: Passport ID Driver's License ARC
Document NumberThe unique number printed on the document.
Country of IssueThe country that issued the document.
Date of IssueThe date the document was issued.
Expiry DateShown in red if expired or expiring within 6 months. Shows - if no expiry applies.
StatusActive Applied for Renewal Inactive
Actions View — Opens the document scan/PDF.
Edit — Update document details.
Delete — Permanently removes the record.
Sorting

Click any column header to sort the list by that column. Click again to reverse the sort order.

Using Filters

Click Document Filters to expand the filter bar. Filter by Holder, Type, Country, and Status. Use Clear All to reset.

Adding and Editing Documents

Click + Add New Passport/ID at the bottom of the list to add a document, or click the Edit button on any row to update it.

1 Holder Name
Select the person this document belongs to. Multiple documents can be added per person.
2 Document Type
Select Passport, ID, Driver's License, Visa, or ARC. This determines the badge colour in the list.
3 Document Number
Enter the unique number exactly as it appears on the document.
4 Country of Issue
Select the country that issued the document.
5 Date of Issue & Expiry Date
Enter both dates. The Expiry Date drives the notification system. Leave blank if the document doesn't expire — it will show as -.
6 Upload Document Scan
Upload a scan or photo (PDF, JPG, or PNG, max 10MB). This can be viewed via the green View button at any time.
Deleting: The red Delete button permanently removes both the record and the uploaded file. This cannot be undone.
Expiry Notifications

Alivente Online automatically monitors all document expiry dates and sends an email on the 1st and 15th of every month listing all documents that have already expired or will expire within the next 6 months.

What triggers a notification?
Already Expired
Expiry date has passed
Expiring Within 6 Months
Less than 6 months until expiry
Notification schedule
1st
Email sent on the 1st of every month — covers all expired and expiring documents.
15th
Email sent on the 15th of every month — a mid-month check covering the same criteria.
No action required: Notifications are fully automatic. Keep your records up to date and the system handles the rest.
Tips and Best Practices
Always upload a scan
A digital copy means you can access documents from anywhere — no need to carry originals for routine reference.
Always enter the expiry date
Without an expiry date the notification system cannot alert you. Always enter it, even for long-validity documents.
Update when documents are renewed
Add the new document record with the new number and expiry date. Consider marking the old one as Inactive rather than deleting to keep a history.
Check red dates before travel
Make it a habit to scan the list before any international travel. Red expiry dates are an immediate visual signal that action is needed.
Use filters for multiple holders
Filter by Holder to view one person's documents at a time. Filter by Type to see all passports or all driver's licences across everyone.
Be careful with Delete
Deleting removes the record and the uploaded file permanently. For old documents, consider setting status to Inactive instead.
