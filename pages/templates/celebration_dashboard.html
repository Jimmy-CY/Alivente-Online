What is the Celebrations Module?

The Celebrations module helps you stay on top of the important dates in your life. Add contacts and their special events — birthdays, namedays, anniversaries, and more — and the system will remind you every day when a celebration is happening.

Key Features
Contact Management
Add contacts with their name, relationship (Family, Friend, Colleague), contact details and notes.
Multiple Event Types
Track Birthdays, Namedays, Anniversaries, and Custom events for each contact.
Daily Email Reminders
Every day, if someone has a celebration, an email is automatically sent listing who is celebrating and what event.
Calendar & Timeline
View all events in a monthly calendar or a chronological timeline — see the full year at a glance.
30-Day Upcoming Widget
The dashboard shows all celebrations in the next 30 days with a countdown so you're never caught off guard.
Excel Import
Bulk import contacts and events from an Excel spreadsheet — ideal for setting up a large contact list quickly.
Daily Reminder: An email is sent automatically every day that has at least one celebration. No action is required — just keep your contacts and events up to date.
Managing Contacts

Each person in your life is stored as a Contact. You then attach events (birthday, nameday, etc.) to each contact. From the Celebration Management page you can view contacts in Detailed or Compact view.

Adding a Contact
Name — The person's full name as you want it to appear throughout the system.
Relationship — Family, Friend, Colleague, or Other. Used to group and organise contacts.
Email & Phone — Optional contact details for your own reference.
Notes — Any additional notes about the contact.
Contact Views
Detailed View
Shows each contact with all their events expanded — ideal for reviewing and managing events.
Compact View
Shows contacts as a grid of name cards — ideal for quickly browsing all your contacts.
Importing from Excel

Click Import from Excel to bulk-add contacts. Your spreadsheet must follow this format:

Required columns:
Column 1: NAME
Column 2: RELATIONSHIP
Column 3: EVENT or EVENT TYPE
Column 4: DATE (month/day only)
Optional columns:
PRIORITY — High/Normal/Low
NOTIFICATION SETTINGS
Enable Skip duplicate contacts to prevent creating duplicates if a contact already exists by name.
Managing Events

Each contact can have multiple events. In the Detailed View, events are shown beneath each contact's name with their date, how many days away they are, and who will receive notifications.

Event Types
Birthday Annual birth date celebration
Nameday Annual name day celebration
Anniversary Wedding or other anniversary
Custom Any other special occasion
Notification Recipients

Each event shows a list of names with checkboxes — these are the family members who will be notified by email when the event occurs. You can control who gets notified per event.

Event Actions
Add a new event to a contact
Edit event details or date
Delete the event
Note: Event dates are annual — only the day and month matter. The year is ignored so events repeat every year automatically.
Calendar and Timeline Views

Click View Events from the dashboard to access the Celebration Calendar. You can switch between two views using the toggle button at the top.

Calendar View
A traditional monthly grid showing all celebrations on their respective dates. Events appear as coloured bars on the day they occur.

Use Previous and Next to navigate between months. Today's date is highlighted in yellow.
Timeline View
A chronological list of all upcoming celebrations grouped by month. Each event shows the contact name, event type badge, exact date, and how many days away it is.

Ideal for seeing what's coming up across the whole year in one scrollable view.
Dashboard Upcoming Widget

The main dashboard always shows the next 30 days of celebrations in a scrollable list with a countdown badge showing how many days until each event. If today is a celebration day it shows "Today!".

Tip: Use the Timeline View to plan ahead and ensure you have time to organise gifts or messages for important upcoming occasions.
How the Notification System Works

The notification system has three layers that all work together to determine who gets notified, about which events, and when.

1
Set up email addresses in Notification Settings
Go to Personal → Notification Settings to register each family member's email address.

TO: Primary recipients — the main people who receive celebration reminders.
CC: Carbon copy recipients — optional additional people to be kept informed.

A family member will never receive a notification unless their email is registered here first.
2
Choose notification timing per event
When adding or editing an event, you set when reminders should be sent under Notification Settings:

Notify 1 week before (sent at 9:00 AM)
Notify 1 day before (sent at 9:00 AM)
Notify same day (sent at 9:00 AM)

You can tick any combination — for example, tick all three for important events like a parent's birthday.
3
Choose who to notify per event ("Notify These People")
Each event has a Notify These People section showing all family members with a tick box next to each name. Tick the family members who have a relationship with this contact and care about this specific event.

For example:
  • Alexandra Manias's Nameday → all 4 family members ticked → everyone notified
  • A distant colleague's birthday → only Demetri ticked → only Demetri notified
The Rule — all three conditions must be met
A family member receives an email notification if and only if:
✓ Their email is registered in Notification Settings
✓ They are ticked in the "Notify These People" section for that event
✓ The notification timing matches (1 week before, 1 day before, or same day)
Fully automatic: Once set up correctly, all notifications are sent automatically at 9:00 AM. No manual action is required on the day.
Tips and Best Practices
Use Excel import to set up quickly
If you have a large contact list, prepare an Excel file with all names and dates and import them in one go. It's much faster than adding contacts one by one.
Add all event types per contact
For Greek contacts, remember to add both their Birthday and Nameday — these are often separate dates. You can add multiple events per contact.
Check the 30-day widget regularly
The dashboard shows upcoming events in the next 30 days. Make it a habit to check it at the start of each month so you can plan ahead for gifts or messages.
Use Timeline View for year planning
The Timeline View shows the entire year ahead chronologically. It's the best way to see "what's coming up" and plan for important occasions months in advance.
Trust the daily email
Once your contacts are set up, the daily email does the work for you. You'll receive a reminder every morning on any day with a celebration — no need to check the system manually.
Use search to find contacts quickly
The search bar on the Celebration Management page filters contacts in real time. Type a name to quickly jump to a specific person and view or edit their events.
