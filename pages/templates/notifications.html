What is this dashboard?

The Notifications Dashboard is a single-glance health check of your property portfolio. It surfaces six key indicators that need attention — from vacant properties and expiring leases through to overdue invoices and pending expenses.

It's the visual companion to the Daily Property Management Report email — the same data, rendered as clickable cards you can drill into.

Live data: The dashboard pulls live data from the database every time it loads, and again whenever you click Refresh Data. There's no cache — what you see is current.
Related: Daily Property Management Report email (configured in Administration → Notification Settings).
The six status cards

Each card shows a count and is colour-coded by urgency. Click any card to see the underlying records.

Vacant Properties

Properties currently without a tenant. Each vacant property is lost income — act fast.

Expiring Leases

Leases coming up for renewal. Drill in to see tenant, lease end date and renewal due date.

Declined Renewals

Tenants who have declined to renew their lease. These properties will need a new tenant before the lease ends.

Overdue Invoices

Tenant invoices past their due date. Drill in to see the amount, the tenant and how many days overdue.

Expenses Waiting for Approval

Expenses submitted by staff that need a supervisor's approval before they can be paid.

Expenses Waiting for Payment

Expenses that have been approved and are now awaiting payment by the finance team.

What the colours mean

Cards change colour based on the count and the severity of the indicator:

Green — All Clear

The count is zero. Nothing to worry about — this category needs no attention.

Yellow — Warning

There are items needing attention soon, but they aren't urgent yet. Used for:

  • Expiring Leases
  • Expenses Waiting for Approval
  • Expenses Waiting for Payment
Red — Urgent

Items that need immediate action — you're losing money or risk losing money. Used for:

  • Vacant Properties
  • Declined Renewals
  • Overdue Invoices
Quick scan: If everything is green you can move on with your day. Any red card — deal with it now.
How to use the dashboard
Click any card

Click a status card to open a popup listing every record in that category — with all the details you need to take action (property name, tenant, dates, amounts, etc.).

Refresh Data

The dashboard loads fresh data when you arrive on the page. Click Refresh Data any time you want to re-pull the numbers (for example, after you've actioned something in another module).

Status badges in detail popups

Inside each detail popup, records are tagged with coloured badges:

  • VACANT / NEED NEW TENANT — urgent action
  • PENDING / PENDING APPROVAL — awaiting next step
  • APPROVED — AWAITING PAYMENT — ready for finance
Back

Returns you to the home dashboard.

Best Practices
  • Make this your morning page — open it first thing each day. A 10-second scan tells you whether anything needs immediate action.
  • Aim for all-green — the goal is six green cards. Each red or yellow card is a piece of work to close out.
  • Click before you act — don't act on the count alone. Click the card first to see which properties / leases / invoices are affected.
  • Refresh after actioning — once you've paid an invoice or approved an expense, click Refresh Data to confirm the count has dropped.
  • Pair with the email report — the Daily Property Management Report email gives you the same data passively. Use email to stay aware on the move; use this dashboard for action.
  • Drill into Overdue Invoices first — this category usually represents real cash you should be collecting. Prioritise red here.
If a card shows zero but you expected items: Click Refresh Data first. If still zero, the underlying record may be in a status that excludes it (e.g. the invoice was just paid, the lease was just renewed). Check the relevant module to verify.
