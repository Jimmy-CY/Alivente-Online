Forwarding automatically

Gmail: Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → Add a forwarding address → {{ address }}. Gmail sends a confirmation to that address; it appears here and in To check with its code and a Confirm in Gmail button. Then make a filter (from: booking.com OR ryanair.com OR …) and choose Forward it to {{ address }}.

Outlook: Settings → Mail → Rules → Add a rule: when the sender contains booking.com (and the others), Forward to {{ address }}.

By hand works just as well: open the email and Forward it to {{ address }}.
