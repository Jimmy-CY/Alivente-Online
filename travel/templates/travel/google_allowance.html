Suggest things to do checks each suggestion on Google – opening days, distance, rating and price. Every check is one Google search. Google lets a project cap searches only per day; here you set Alivente’s own monthly allowance too. When either is used up, suggestions still come, just not checked on Google, until the next day or the 1st. At 80% of the month you get one email.

Google’s free amount for these searches is about 1,000 a month; beyond it, roughly $35 per 1,000. Google’s own cap on the key’s project is 100 a day – a higher day here does nothing unless Google’s is raised too.
