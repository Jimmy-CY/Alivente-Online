Install it

On Android, in Chrome: the menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen / Install app. On an iPhone, in Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. It stays signed in for 30 days on the phone. After Travel is updated, the app fetches its new look by itself the next time it opens with a signal.

Today

While you are away: today’s and tomorrow’s bookings — flights, check-ins and check-outs, the car. Before a trip: how many days to go and the first day. Deadlines to cancel for free or to pay show under Coming up.

Passes

Three tabs at the top. Next: the flight you need now, your own pass first — swipe for the others on the same flight. Coming up: the flights after it; tap one to open its pass. Used: every pass, kept for good, stamped USED with the barcode greyed — Show the barcode anyway brings it back. A pass is used six hours after its flight was due to land, so a delay does not take it away; on a connection the next pass takes over at the first flight’s landing time (Then and Earlier today keep both one tap away). Done with this pass moves on at once; Not flown yet brings a used pass back. After the flight, Add to Google Wallet explains that airlines stop offering the pass to Wallet. The barcode is drawn again from the airline’s own code, so the scanner reads it off the screen. Turn the brightness up. While Passes is open the screen stays on, and turning the phone does not flip the pass. Passes you have opened stay on the phone and work with no signal.

Getting bookings in

Forward the booking email to {{ address }}, or on Android use Share → Travel on an email, a PDF ticket or a screenshot. It is read in a minute and waits under To check. A later email about a booking you have updates it by itself.

Calendar

On Trips, tap Calendar to see your trips on a month: each trip a coloured bar, paler once it is over, with a plane on the days you fly. It opens on this month; swipe or tap ‹ › for the next or last month, or pick a month and year. Tap a trip to open its itinerary. The trips of the month are listed underneath. List goes back; the phone remembers which you used.

Plan my days

On Things to do, Plan my days spreads what has no day over the trip. Three steps: can things already on a day move (a booked ticket always stays), which mornings, afternoons or evenings to keep free, and your pace, getting around and must-dos. It works out where each place is, its hours, how long a visit takes and when it is quiet, then fits them in near each other, never when closed, and only on a day you are in that country (going by your flights). Your plan shows each day; tap Move to change a day, then Accept plan – nothing changes before that. Undo plan stays until the trip ends or you plan again. Added more afterwards? Fit them in puts the new ones into the gaps.

Suggest things to do

On Things to do, Suggest things to do gives you ideas for the cities you are staying in. Step 1: tick the cities (from your hotels) or add a day trip. Step 2: what you like – museums, parks, neighbourhoods, viewpoints and more – and best known, a mix or more local. About 12 ideas a city, each with a short description, how long it takes, the price and a note for your dates. Tick Restaurants or Cafés for the city’s top ten of each, with rating and price. Set How far from the centre? in km if you like. Everything is checked on Google: a card is greyed out, with the reason, when it is closed on every day you are in that city or further than your limit (day trips too) – you can still tick it. These are kept apart at the end of the city, under Outside what you asked for – tap it to open. The round icon at the top right of each suggestion, and beside each heading, shows what kind it is – a star for must-sees, a palette for museums, a tree for parks, a signpost for streets, binoculars for viewpoints, a cup for cafés and so on. Nothing already on your list is suggested. Tick the ones you want and tap Add to Things to do – each is looked up for its address and hours. Not interested hides one; Show me more asks for more without repeats – your ticks stay, and the new ones are marked New. Your suggestions stay on the trip to come back to.

Google checks left (at the top) says how many Google checks this month and today still has; each suggestion checked uses one. When they run out, suggestions still come, just not checked on Google – they’re checked automatically the next morning, or tap Check them now. One Google can’t find at all says Not found on Google in grey – you can still tick it.

Delete several things to do

On Things to do, tap Select to delete, tick the ones to go and tap Delete – it asks first. Cancel leaves them all.

Places and the PDF

On a trip, Itinerary is every day with its bookings and places; Things to do is places with no day yet — choose a day to put one on it. A day is in time order, but a hotel check-in or car pick-up on a day you fly goes after the flight (After you land, ~23:45). To put a day in your own order, hold the grip (⋮⋮) on the right of a row and drag it up or down — the others slide aside, and the page scrolls when you reach the edge. It saves when you let go (Undo for a few seconds), and is kept for everyone on the trip; Back to automatic under the day undoes it. Add one by name with + Things to do (short or misspelled is fine), or in Google Maps tap Share → Travel; if two or three places could be meant, pick the one you meant. A restaurant booking for a place in Things to do takes its place. PDF at the bottom of a trip: choose Summary or Detailed and send it to WhatsApp or mail.

The weather

While you are away, Today starts with the weather where you are: the city of tonight’s hotel, the conditions, the high and low, the chance and amount of rain, the wind, and a line for tomorrow. Before a trip it shows the first day’s forecast once it is about two weeks away. On a trip’s Itinerary, each day the forecast reaches has a small weather chip. Before anything is booked, it follows your Things to do. Nothing to set up; with no signal there is simply no weather.

Budget and spending

Bookings are what they cost; the budget is for everything else – meals, taxis, tickets at the door, shopping. On a trip’s Costs tab, Set a daily spending budget: a day’s or the holiday’s – type one and the other is worked out. + Spending (on Costs, and on Today while you are away): type the amount and tap Add – euros, today and an empty note are filled in. A meal at a restaurant you booked goes here too. Only a day of the trip can be chosen; tap a spending to change or delete it. Any on a day outside the trip is listed apart – move it or delete it. Today shows what you spent today against the day’s budget and whether you are ahead or behind plan; Costs shows a bar for each day, red when it went over, and what a day keeps you on budget for the rest of the trip. After a trip, Costs says how it came out – €780 less than budgeted or €200 more – and warns if days have no spending recorded. Daily spending is part of the trip total. Only the trip’s owner adds spending; the others see it only if you share the costs.

Between trips

With nothing coming up, Today shows your last trip and the ones before it, and Trips shows the past trips. Trips are always listed latest first. At the top of Trips: All, Upcoming and Past, each with its number in a small badge, and the Year and Country menus. A picked year or country shows as a chip — tap its ✕ to clear it. They combine, so Past with Greece shows only your past trips to Greece. A trip that has ended with something still To pay says so — settle up on the trip. Trips → Compare: tick two or more trips to see their costs side by side.

Changing things

Add, edit, share and merge trips on the full site, under Personal → Travel. The phone is for the trip itself.
