Install it

On Android, in Chrome: the menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen / Install app. On an iPhone, in Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. It stays signed in for 30 days on the phone.

Today

While you are away: today’s and tomorrow’s bookings — flights, check-ins and check-outs, the car. Before a trip: how many days to go and the first day. Deadlines to cancel for free or to pay show under Coming up.

Passes

Every boarding pass to come, one a screen — swipe for the next person’s. It always opens on the next pass you need: once a flight has landed, the next flight’s pass is the one on screen, and earlier ones are a swipe back. The barcode is drawn again from the airline’s own code, so the scanner reads it off the screen. Turn the brightness up. While Passes is open the screen stays on, and turning the phone does not flip the pass. Passes you have opened stay on the phone and work with no signal.

Getting bookings in

Forward the booking email to {{ address }}, or on Android use Share → Travel on an email, a PDF ticket or a screenshot. It is read in a minute and waits under To check. A later email about a booking you have updates it by itself.

Places and the PDF

On a trip, Itinerary is every day with its bookings and places; Things to do is places with no day yet — choose a day to put one on it. Add a place by name (short or misspelled is fine), or in Google Maps tap Share → Travel; if two or three places could be meant, pick the one you meant. A restaurant booking for a place in Things to do takes its place. PDF at the bottom of a trip: choose Summary or Detailed and send it to WhatsApp or mail.

Changing things

Add, edit, share and merge trips on the full site, under Personal → Travel. The phone is for the trip itself.
