Put Travel on your phone

  1. Scan the code with your phone’s camera and tap the link that appears. (Or type alivente.online/travel/m/ into Chrome on the phone.)
  2. Sign in if it asks — with your usual Alivente email and password.
  3. In Chrome, tap the three dots ⋮ at the top right.
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home screen (on some phones it says Install app).
  5. Choose Install (not Create shortcut), then Install again to confirm.
  6. Open Travel from your home screen. It stays signed in for 30 days.

iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
