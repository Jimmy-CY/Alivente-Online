Back On The Right Edge

H2b — one rule in base puts a lone Back back on the right on a phone; two Celebrations pages give up a crowded bar and a loose search field.

The dashed pink outline is drawn by this sheet, not by the site: it marks the Back button, which has no border of its own and whose arrow is an icon-font glyph, so it would otherwise be invisible here. The number under each phone shot is Back’s measured distance from the right-hand end of its own action bar, and whether any control’s label is wider than the button holding it.

Empty white boxes are the fixture, not the page. Font Awesome is not loaded here, so every icon-only control — More, Filter, Back below 768 px, and every row action — renders as its own empty outline. They carry glyphs on the site.

The phone pair is the round. Desktop is shown to prove the rule is phone-only: on the four pages whose template did not change, the two desktop shots are the same picture. Rendered at 1280 px and 390 px from base’s backup stylesheet (before) and base’s live one (after) — and one branch of each {% if %}, the way Django renders it.
