The Hub Gives Up Its Bar

G3b-2 — the Personal recipes hub did not lack an action bar. It had written one, faithfully, in its own stylesheet — and sixteen of its rules were base’s, re-typed.

The marker classes were already right. Every control carried .action-primary, .action-secondary or .action-back — but the paint came from Bootstrap underneath, so the bar read as green and blue buttons wearing house names. Taking btn-success, btn-primary and btn-secondary off is what makes the names mean something.

A tap target, for free. The local rules sized these controls at 38 px on a phone; base sizes them at 44, which is its tap-target standard. Deleting the copies raises six controls without this round mentioning height once.

The Favourites toggle keeps its colour — red when the filter is on, outlined when it is off. The colour IS the state, and Show-ButtonDrift already records that category and leaves it alone. So does this.

Rendered from the backup (before) and from the changed file (after), same stylesheet, at 1280 px and 390 px. Icons are missing because Font Awesome is not loaded in the fixture.
