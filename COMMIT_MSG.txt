Administration stage D: one panel title, one size, and the tag stops deciding

base declared .form-section-title with no font-size, so the heading TAG
decided how big a panel title was. 34 titles on 19 screens now carry one
tag and one class, 30 page-local rules went with them, and the two that
scope the component to a particular panel stayed.

ONE COMPONENT, FOUR SIZES

Measured with Bootstrap loaded and base's size taken away, the four tags
these titles actually used:

    h2, no class at all   13 titles    32px
    h3                                 28px
    h5.form-section-title  6 titles    20px
    h6.form-section-title 12 titles    16px

Nothing chose those numbers. They are what the browser does with whichever
heading a page happened to reach for. base says 16px now, with a 9px
bottom padding and a 2px accent rule, and the tag carries no appearance.

MY OWN MEASUREMENT OF THIS WAS WRONG, AND WRITING THE CONTROL FOUND IT

The round's first draft said the sizes were 13.28px, 14px, 16px, 16.8px
and 16px, and that two of them fell at or BELOW the 14px field labels -
"Personal Information" smaller than "First Name" beneath it.

Those were measured WITHOUT Bootstrap. The CDN is unreachable from the
sandbox, the stylesheet never loaded, and every figure was a browser
default. With Bootstrap loaded nothing was smaller than its labels.

    A rendering test that renders without the stylesheet the page
    actually has measures nothing.

Same fault as the btn-sm measurement in the button round, five days apart.
The spread is real and is reason enough; the sentence about the labels was
not, and both docstrings now say so. Section 5's control is what produced
the four true numbers.

THREE HEADINGS THE ROUND SAID IT WOULD NOT TOUCH, AND WAS TOUCHING

The prose named .lines-title and .pi-section-title as out of scope -
"they sit OUTSIDE any .form-card". Measured, that is true of one of four:

    customer_invoice_form  .lines-title       8623, card 2096-7575   OUT
    physical_invoice_edit  .lines-title       2724, card 1696-5880   IN
    tenant_add             .pi-section-title  9847, card 1187-14893  IN
    tenant_edit            .pi-section-title 11805, card 2371-17054  IN

Three of them sit inside a panel doing a panel title's job under another
name. The sweeper tests containment and was right; the prose was a list
written from one page's shape, and the list lost. They are swept.

    A list chosen from a corpus you cannot see is a list.
    A rule is a standard.

Second time this week that sentence has been the finding.

AND THEIR CSS WAS BEING LEFT BEHIND

The sweeper renamed their markup and deleted nothing, because the
deletable set was a constant - TITLE_CLASSES - rather than a question
about this page. Six rules would have shipped matching nothing. Dead, not
wrong, which is why nothing caught it.

The set is per page now: a rule may only go where base has taken its job
over ON THIS PAGE. .lines-title rules go on physical_invoice_edit, where
the markup was swept, and STAY on customer_invoice_form, where it was not.

BASE ALREADY OWNED THE ICON - THE ROUND ONLY STOPPED REPEATING IT

17 of the 34 titles put a Font Awesome icon before the words and every one
said the colour again: 13 as an inline style attribute, 6 as a page rule
`.pi-section-title i { color: #0e7c8b }`. base has declared
`.form-section-title i { color: var(--alv-accent); margin-right: 6px }`
since the form-components round on 16 Sep, and --alv-accent IS #0e7c8b.

So all 19 restatements say exactly what base says, and deleting them moves
nothing on screen. Section 6 measures that rather than asserting it. The
patcher now refuses to write if that rule is missing - not an addition, a
dependency made explicit.

WHAT IS REPORTED RATHER THAN SWEPT, each by name and with its reason

  edit_asset.html            one form-card holds the whole form and its
                             first heading is the record's name. Sweeping

  customer_invoice_form      .lines-title, genuinely outside the card
  property_assets            .form-section-heading, a second level of
                             structure under a panel that has a title
  19 form-cards              carry no heading at all. A panel without a
                             title is not necessarily wrong, and this
                             round did not invent one.

test_panel_title.py    39 checks. Section 4 is the one that earns its
                       keep, and it exists because this round nearly
                       shipped the fault it checks for: does any page
                       style a class its own markup no longer wears? It
                       fails on what THIS round could have orphaned and

                       before the round had even run, half of them false
                       positives from `class="status-badge status-{{
                       pi.status }}"`, where the name is built at render

FOUND, NOT FIXED: 282 page-local rules across the system name a class no
markup wears. Left by earlier rounds that moved markup onto base and did
not follow with the CSS. The suite lists them every run. That is a round
of its own, and a large one.

Three more faults were caught by a fixture rather than by the gate:
patch_base inserted its declarations OUTSIDE the braces on a rule written
on one line, reported an icon rule it had not added, and said "patched" on
every subsequent run; and two control thresholds were numbers I could not
verify in both a full and a partial checkout.
